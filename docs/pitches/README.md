# Concept pitches

Seven designers were asked to pitch one concept each for the same brief, each through a different
design lens. Six pitches came back; the seventh (the "space physics / gravity" lens) never returned
and the panel was stopped after 57 minutes, so the two judge agents never ran and the choice was made
by the orchestrator from these six.

| Lens | Pitch | One line |
|---|---|---|
| spectacle | **BLUESHIFT** (chosen) | Outrun a nova down the axis of a cylinder city: roll the tube, flip through its core, breach walls on the beat |
| rhythm | NEEDLE DROP | You are the needle on a planet-sized record and the bass only drops when you slam space |
| arcade | PRISM BREAK | Flip polarity at the last instant to smash through walls; bank your comet of loot before a mistake scatters it |
| flow | NIGHTSIDE | Swing between satellites over a ring city, outrunning the sunrise; speed is your life |
| combat | VOID RONIN | Enemies are the only road across the void; every kill refuels the blink to the next |
| wildcard | BACKSPIN | Hold a key to un-explode a dead city in a bubble around you, then drop the explosion on everything inside |

Why BLUESHIFT: its three verbs only exist because of depth, each has a big payoff even on a miss,
the guaranteed "whoa" at 9.4 s needs no skill, and the whole game is a table (9 cells x 16th
notes), so a bot can prove levels fair and one pass can polish it. Ideas it shares with the others
(gear = music layer = speed, a chasing deadline instead of a health bar, beat-locked hazards) showed
up independently in three or more pitches, which is its own kind of vote.
